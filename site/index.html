Where Hush came from

It started with one loud laptop.

My partner is a fantastic creative who uses her Mac for everything. For months it was hot, loud, and slow, with nothing on screen to explain why.

One evening I went digging and found the culprit: a background task called Adobe Crash Processor, quietly helping itself to most of her machine.

So I built Hush and pointed it at the noise. The task keeps doing its job — just at a volume her laptop can live with.

Her Mac went back to being the thing she bought: sleek, quiet, and capable.

Hush is that fix, wrapped up for anyone who’d rather make things than manage a machine.
